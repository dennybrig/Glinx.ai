## The investment story

Glinx aims to make a company self-aware in an operational sense: understand its purpose and condition, explain what is happening, act within human authority, and learn from measured results. Each evaluated outcome updates the company model and the next decision.

The deck uses Denny's five-layer operating model: **Company organs → Connector fabric → Evidence plane → Company Connectome → Glinx cognition.** It covers what Glinx is, why it matters, who would use it, the recursive improvement loop, use cases, product scope, commercial strategy, market context, competition, roadmap, founder fit and the investment milestones.
