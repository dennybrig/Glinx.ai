# Glinx Company Discovery — investor presentation

An editable 15-slide investor briefing: 13 main slides and 2 appendix slides.

## Files

- [PowerPoint deck](Glinx-Company-Discovery-Investor-Deck.pptx) — editable slide text and tables, with presenter notes.
- [PDF deck](Glinx-Company-Discovery-Investor-Deck.pdf) — a fixed-layout copy for presenting and sharing.
- [Presenter notes](Glinx-Company-Discovery-Presenter-Notes.md) — slide-by-slide talking points and links to supporting product documentation.

## Present it

Allow approximately 8–10 minutes for the main story, then use the [five-minute product walkthrough](../INVESTOR-DEMO.md). Slides 14–15 are reference material for questions.

The story covers the customer problem, the owner-controlled discovery workflow, supported source types, the fictional Alder Forge example, evidence quality, current engineering proof, the broader vision, and a proposed first company pilot.

The [hosted demo](https://glinx-discovery.dennybrig.chatgpt.site) and this repository are private. Present the demo from your authorized account; download the PDF if you need to share the presentation independently.

## Collaborate

Edit the PowerPoint in PowerPoint or compatible presentation software. Keep its presenter notes and this Markdown companion aligned. Submit slide or narrative changes through a pull request. Regenerate the PDF when the PowerPoint changes.

The visual style uses Glinx branding with restrained crimson, charcoal, and white, and Arial typography. It does not imply affiliation with Bain or PwC.

## Basis and scope

Prepared in September 2026 against software commit [`8e49df9`](https://github.com/dennybrig/Glinx.ai/commit/8e49df99fe437d0452892e5034a3c0ca97e56a8c).

- Alder Forge and its data are fictional demonstration material.
- Existing capabilities are separated from future product work.
- Customer value, manufacturing focus, commercial terms, and pilot evaluation methods are proposals to validate.
- The deck does not claim customer traction, measured financial results, whole-company discovery from one computer, or autonomous business decisions.
